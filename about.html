--- about us ---

We Serve Delicious Food SINCE 2013

At Folk & Flame, we believe in serving more than just food. We serve happiness. Every dish leaves our kitchen the way it would leave a home kitchen — unhurried, seasoned by hand, and built around ingredients bought fresh that morning.

"Every flame in this kitchen still burns
for the same reason it did in 2013"

What started as a single family recipe book in 2013 has grown into a full kitchen, but the rule hasn't changed: nothing goes to a
table that the chef wouldn't serve their own family first.
